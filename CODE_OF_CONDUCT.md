# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for everyone involved in Mboni.

This applies to project team members, maintainers, contributors, reviewers, mentors, partners, and other participants.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus discussions on the project and its technical or organizational goals.
- Respect different perspectives and approaches.
- Collaborate in good faith.
- Give appropriate credit to other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or intimidation.
- Deliberate disruption of project activities.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorization.
- Any other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Reporting

Concerns about conduct should be reported privately to a project maintainer:

- [@AimableUK](https://github.com/AimableUK)
- [@EnockHumure](https://github.com/EnockHumure)

Contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile and ask for a private way to share the details. Do not post the concern in a public issue or Pull Request.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. If a report involves a maintainer, send it to the other maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may respond to unacceptable behavior by giving a warning, asking for a change in behavior, removing or editing comments or contributions, or temporarily or permanently removing a person from participation in the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related communication, contributions, Pull Requests, discussions, meetings, and other activities associated with the project.
