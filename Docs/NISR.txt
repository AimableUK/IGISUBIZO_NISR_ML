But rather than creating another LinkedIn-like site, we could investigate:

Can national statistics identify which skills/occupations are in demand in which regions/sectors?

LFS + establishment census + earnings + hours + employment status + sector

The strongest employment opportunity isn't necessarily:

"Build an AI job-matching platform."

That's too obvious.

The deeper opportunity appears to be:

Build better intelligence about Rwanda's labour market so governments, training institutions and other actors can understand where workforce supply, skills, business demand and employment outcomes do or don't align.
